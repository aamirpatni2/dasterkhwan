Our Story

A family table, open to the whole city

Karachi Dastarkhwan began as a small nihari stall near Empress Market. Today we serve the same recipes in a warm dining room, still cooked overnight in copper degs and finished over charcoal.

A dastarkhwan is the cloth spread for a shared meal. That is what we offer: generous portions, honest spices and a seat for everyone.
